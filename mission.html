About ANZEP

Our Mission

Why we exist, what we stand for, and where we're headed.

Mission statement

Connecting researchers.
Generating impact.

Our mission is to bring together environmental psychology researchers across Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand, fostering connections, collaborations, and knowledge exchange within ANZEP and beyond to generate more impact.

What this means in practice

Three pillars of our work

🤝

Connection

Environmental psychology researchers across Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand are doing important, complementary work — often without knowing who else is out there. ANZEP exists to change that, creating genuine connections across institutions, career stages, and sub-disciplines.

🔬

Collaboration

Great research rarely happens in isolation. By building a formal network, we create the conditions for cross-institutional collaborations, joint grant applications, and shared research agendas that no single group could pursue alone.

🌏

Impact

Knowledge exchange only matters if it translates into real-world outcomes. We are committed to building a network that moves environmental psychology research from the lab and the journal page into communities, policy, and practice across the region.

Background

How ANZEP came to be

The community of social psychologists conducting research in environmental psychology across Australia and New Zealand has been steadily growing. Researchers at universities from Brisbane to Christchurch, from Wellington to Perth, have been independently advancing the field — studying climate beliefs, pro-environmental behaviour, misinformation, and the psychology of sustainability.

ANZEP was formally established to build on that momentum — to create a network that reflects and amplifies this growing community. Our first pre-conference, held in November 2025 at the University of Melbourne, brought together researchers from across the region to share insights, identify opportunities, and co-develop a shared research agenda focused on real-world impact.

The network is researcher-led and volunteer-run, coordinated by a Steering Committee drawn from universities across both countries. We are committed to being an inclusive, cross-Tasman community that values diverse research approaches and supports researchers at all career stages.
